Fair use

So that bot-link keeps working for everyone, these limits apply to every account:

  • Messages your chats sent that are still stored on the server: up to 64 MB per account. Over it, a new message is not sent until older ones are read or expire. When the whole server stores 8192 MB, no new message is stored until space comes back.
  • Rooms: up to 20 rooms you own at a time, and up to 30 new rooms a day.
  • In each room: up to 20 chats and up to 10 people.
  • Sending: up to 30 messages a minute and 1000 a day from each chat, and 300 a minute in each room.
  • New accounts: up to 10 an hour and 30 a day from one address, and 1000 an hour and 5000 a day in total.
